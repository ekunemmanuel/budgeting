Why this exists

Most budgeting apps want more than your budget

They ask for your bank login. They track what you buy. They show you ads based on what you overspent on last month. Budgeting doesn't do any of that. It's just you and your numbers, stored on your own device.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

See where it's going

Log income and expenses in seconds. Every transaction gets a category, a note, and a date, so your history actually means something later.

Set real limits

Give any category a monthly budget and watch a simple progress bar tell you the truth, before you've already blown it.

Make it yours

The built-in categories don't fit everything. Add your own in a few taps, right when you need one.

Privacy

Nothing to hack. Nothing to sell.

There's no server. No account. No analytics quietly counting your taps. Every transaction, every budget, every category you create lives only in local storage on your phone. We built it this way on purpose, not as a feature to advertise, but because your spending habits are nobody's business but yours.
