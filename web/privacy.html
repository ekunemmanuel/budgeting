Privacy Policy

Last updated September 12, 2026

This app was built on one rule: your financial data is yours alone. Everything you enter, every transaction, every budget, every category, stays on your device. It is never collected, never transmitted, and never seen by us or anyone else.

No account required No data collected No ads or trackers Works fully offline

01 Information we collect

None. This app does not collect, transmit, or have access to any personal or financial information. There is no sign-up, no account, and no server this app talks to.

Everything you type (transaction amounts, notes, category names, budget limits) is written only to your device's local storage. We never see it.

02 Internet access

The app does not require or request internet access to function. It was designed to work completely offline, and no data ever leaves your phone.

03 Third parties

No analytics, no advertising networks, no crash-reporting services, no third-party SDKs of any kind are included in this app. Nothing about your usage is shared with anyone, because nothing is collected in the first place.

04 Your data, your control

  • All data lives in local storage on your device only.
  • There is currently no cloud backup. If you uninstall the app or lose your device, that data is gone with it, and we can't recover it for you.
  • Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it permanently deletes everything you've entered.

05 Children's privacy

Because this app collects no information from any user, it does not knowingly collect information from children either. It is not directed at, and does not target, children specifically.

06 Changes to this policy

If this app ever changes how it handles data, for example by adding an optional cloud backup, this page will be updated first, with a new "last updated" date above, before that change ships.
